Mathematical Health Score Formula

The SEO Health Score evaluates technical perfection across all crawled pages on a 0 to 100 scale. Each issue type deducts weighted points normalized by total site size:

Total Penalty = (Critical × 5.0) + (Warnings × 2.0) + (Info × 0.5) Penalty Per Page = Total Penalty ÷ Total Crawled Pages Final Score = Max( 0 , 100 - (5.0 × Penalty Per Page) )
